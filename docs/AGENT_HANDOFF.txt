CURRENT RULES
- Win by army elimination. A captured king pays $500 but does not decide the game.
  No check/checkmate, standard castling or en passant in the actual match.
- Seven distinct random EMPTY squares start as HIDDEN mysteries. No idle stars
  or hover hints may reveal them. First discovery opens Bazaar; second unlocks
  cards. Campaign unlocks persist into later encounters, which seed seven fresh
  hidden mysteries. The first campaign battle also starts with systems locked.
- Discovery stars spray/spin UNDER the piece and fade within half a tile. Begin
  white, then choose EXACTLY ONE color: green for positive OR red for negative.
  Neutral stays white. The ~1.45s effect begins on arrival, uses varied star sizes
  and unsymmetrical radii, and remains visible outside the piece silhouette.
  Keep positive/negative visual test samples apart; never overlap them on one tile.
- Money accrues before Bazaar unlock, but no treasury labels, capture amounts,
  king bounty copy or Chronicle financial hints may show beforehand. Reveal old
  amounts retroactively after unlocking; never erase financial events from history.
- End Turn Early is invisible until player turn ten (ply 9), and during replay.
- Clicking an enemy pins its details and previews potential moves, respecting its
  statuses and tether while ignoring the acting court's shot/bonus locks. Preview
  must not change RNG, live turn, pieces or revision, or allow commanding the enemy.
- Cards never draw automatically each turn. Once unlocked, maintain three visible
  card-draw markers on available empty squares. Reaching one draws a card and
  relocates it. Replace overwritten/removed markers. Robotic Arm doubles a tile
  claim. Greed draws two, Card Pack five. Campaign Scholar's Charm is a specific
  starting-card reward after cards are unlocked.
- Hold more than four during your turn. Resolve a move/purchase/turn end first,
  then let the OWNER choose extra discards down to four before the opponent begins.
  Pending discard state survives saves. Bots choose their own legal discards.
- Lasso targets TWO OPPOSING pieces within four squares, restricting separation.
- Scavenge selects the furthest-forward friendly carrier. Move THAT piece home
  onto either of its two home ranks within five rounds for $100. Ordinary pawns
  cannot retreat without a movement card. Mark that carrier clearly.
- Bazaar is hidden until unlocked. Paid visits cost a turn even without buying.
  Every fifth round grants one free browse; buying still ends the turn.
- Small visit warning includes Don't warn me again; confirmed opt-out persists.
  Require two clicks for Bazaar defaults ON. While warnings remain enabled, one
  click opens the warning. After opt-out, first click says Click again to open;
  a second click within 3.5 seconds enters. Reset on turn change/timeout. Both
  protections are configurable. With both disabled, entry uses a single click.
- Standard duels expand to 20x20 at round 20; campaign battles stay 8x8.

CURRENT PRESENTATION
- Readable Atkinson body text, Railway 1901 headings and Krefeld accents. Avoid
  thin 1080p text and desktop widgets. Preserve restrained motion and pixel art.
- Main menu: name, play choices, difficulty, Continue and Settings. No old slogans,
  Archive or How to Play links there. Faint drifting chessboards in the background
  play ordinary chess moves at randomized slow intervals; they use separate state.
  Three closely spaced rows of five boards fill the background.
- Escape opens compact pause with title/mode, Resume, Settings, rules, Archive,
  saving and main menu. Offline pause/replay stops bot/clock; online stays live.
- The board always fits: no zoom, pan or Reset View. Pixel board/rotate icon flips
  it. Coordinates show BOTH ranks and files and default OFF.
- Player cards fan out, tuck down and lift just enough to read. Distinct colors,
  two-line long titles, flavor bigger than short mechanics. Full mechanics belong
  in Particulars and Chronicle tooltips, without parentheses. Tiny translucent
  position numbers replace circle badges. No permanent Your Hand/activation label.
  Each of the 21 cards has its own original detailed 48px pixel illustration in
  ui/card_art.gd. Draws fade/slide in from the right, preserving existing card nodes.
- Single click inspects. Double click or drag to a generous blue PLAY zone plays
  or begins targeting. Number keys 1-9 latch a card to the mouse until a click.
  Red hand zone or Escape returns it. Clicking another menu cancels pickup and
  still operates that menu. Held cards pivot at top middle with light lag/wiggle.
  Return/play animation starts at the actual pose. Both actors' cards settle at
  the BOARD center, not screen center.
- Opponent has full-sized upside-down backs, no inspection/activation, subtle
  forward hover nudge and a small bordered count. Hide number and box when empty.
- Click Chronicle over Particulars. Pages represent player turns, plus Starting
  Position. Outcomes go in row titles, not a separate detail panel. More than six
  actions show a scrollbar; short turns hide it. W/S choose current-turn actions,
  A/D change turns; arrows/Home/End also navigate. The live Chronicle title is large
  and yellow, with a SMALL title-only tilt target. Remove bullet history/caption.
  Fixed left/right arrows stay at the same position in live/replay: left enters
  the preceding move directly, right is muted while live and exits replay when
  reaching the newest move. Keep the live match unmodified throughout.
- Hover temporarily previews history; leaving restores the clicked/pinned frame.
  Click pins. Highlight origins/destinations/captures/effects and show full played
  card tooltips. Heist signs are from viewer perspective. Large bold translucent
  REPLAY watermark. Completely hide Bazaar and End Turn. Return to Live restores
  the UNMODIFIED match. Replay must never submit actions or mutate live state.
- Tips NEVER yield to context. They cycle every eighteen seconds unless the TIP
  PANEL is hovered. Low-contrast grey fuse, adjacent arrows at its end, current/total
  count. Clicking arrows resets the fuse. Put targeting prompts in Particulars.
- Clickable text uses tiny hover tilt/underline and a hand cursor. Queen shares
  the king body with a distinct crown, avoiding the previous cactus silhouette.

ARCHITECTURE
godot/game/match.gd is the sole authoritative transactional model. It handles
targeting, effects, seeded RNG, hidden mysteries/draw markers, snapshots and history.
catalog.gd/content.json contain 21 cards, 74 items and 17 units. Update the generator
tools/build-content.py together with JSON. short is compact card copy; description
is the full rule. bot.gd evaluates detached snapshots with four bounded levels.
campaign.gd manages six battles, survivors, routes and rewards. network.gd manages
ENet roles, revisions and private snapshots. app.gd composes runtime UI and flow.
ui/battlefield.gd draws the board, particles, coordinates and replay marks.
ui/hand.gd, playing_card.gd and card_drag.gd own fans, card faces/backs and pickup.
ui/chronicle.gd owns turn groups, action outcomes and replay navigation.
ui/menu_boards.gd has an independent decorative ordinary-chess simulation.
ui/pixels.gd contains original generated silhouettes; ui/card_art.gd draws cards.
ui/hints.gd owns the carousel. ui/flip_icon.gd draws a nearly closed self-pointing
pixel rotation arrow around its chessboard.
Keep authoritative rules out of presentation widgets.
