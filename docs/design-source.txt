Shop Stuff

-Shop opens after a tile activates it. This tile is guaranteed in the first 4 tiles. 

-Refresh in shop for cheap price at any area in shop.

-Shop doesn't have unlimited access. Spend your money while you can. 

-Access shop at any time. But, be wise, as it will use your turn if you visit the shop. 

-Every 5 turns, there will be a shop turn. No penalty for not buying anything. 

-You can't battle anything in the shop

-Can only move with your piece like it can normally move

-Uses a turn if you buy something, and you leave immediately when you do

-Walls that can't be destroyed by regular means prevent pieces from going past

-Shop is split into 2 areas. Manual activation, and the more permanent passive items. Shop will NOT include items that automatically trigger activation. It is passive items and manual activation items, no automatic activation. Those are special, and limited to tiles.

-Shop items will not be debuffs. Play offensively and reactively, not with control.
